Signing the package
-------------------
The certificate subject must equal the Publisher in app\ClinicAVT.App\Package.appxmanifest,
or signtool fails with 0x8007000B.

  Get-ChildItem Cert:\CurrentUser\My | Format-List Subject, Thumbprint
  signtool sign /fd SHA256 /tr <timestamp server> /td SHA256 /sha1 <thumbprint> ClinicAVT_<version>_x64.msix
  signtool verify /pa /v ClinicAVT_<version>_x64.msix
