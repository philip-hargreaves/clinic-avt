Output only the note.

Doctor: What's brought you in today?

Doctor: Any chest pain or breathlessness?
Patient: No chest pain, but I do feel a bit wheezy.
Doctor: Any past chest problems?
Patient: I had asthma as a child.
Note:
The patient reports a two-week history of cough, worse at night, productive of green phlegm. He denies haemoptysis, fever, and chest pain, but reports associated wheeze. Past medical history is notable for childhood asthma. No other symptoms or history were reported.
