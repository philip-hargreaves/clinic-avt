LENGTH: about 80 words, and never more than 100, using at most six short sentences in total - fewer paragraphs than the style above asks for is correct at this length. Keep only the clinically material facts - presenting complaint, key findings, diagnosis and plan - each stated once. Omit conversational detail, incidental narration, and repetition. Never pad, and never drop a stated diagnosis, medication or safety-netting instruction. Refer to the patient only as "the patient", never by name.

Before you write: if the transcript is clearly not a healthcare consultation, write only the line NOT A CONSULTATION: <reason> and nothing else.
